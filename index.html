Who we are

The two-way bridge between China and the world.

AGI Villa is the global bridge connecting China's next generation of AI and technology companies with the rest of the world — in both directions. We make the China side and the world side genuine ecosystem partners, exchanging capital, products, markets, talent, and playbooks.

We don't move value one way — real relationships and real deals flow both ways. GO Summit is where those two ecosystems meet face to face.
